The Mom Test

Thesis in short:

  • A false positive from a potential end user is far worse than no data at all. Talk about their lives, you don't want them to even know you are trying to sell a product.

Ideas I'll actually use:

  1. Compliments are bad (or at least should be ignored.)
  2. No need for setting up business meetings, just run into them, be involved in their world.
  3. The conversation should not feel like you are trying to sell a product. Phrases:
    • "Why do you bother to X" (X = slow work around to a problem)
    • "What are the implications for that?" (is this REALLY a problem?)
    • "Talk me through the last time it happened"
    • "How are you dealing with this now?"
    • "Who else should I talk to?"
  4. Never to into pitch mode
  5. Never expose your ego
  6. Start the conversations very broad, let the end user zoom in. Let them tell you what part of the problem really burns them.
  7. Prepare a list of questions for each end user you are discussing with
