Disciplined Entrepreneurship

You must be passionate, and understand the space. A founding team should have someone technologically savvy, someone good at marketing/ sales and someone who can manage community and design.

Group 1. (Who is the customer?)

  • Market segmentation: Brainstorm side list of possible market opportunities. Done before beachhead selection
  • Select a beachhead market: Choosing a tiny, very specific market to start off and dominate to validate ideas
  • Build an end user profile: Priorities, watering holes, cares about X over Y...
  • Calculate TAM (for beachhead): Used to understand if good enough opportunity
  • Profile and persona for beachhead market: What is their name, age, habits, favourite food, etc. Actual person.
  • Identify your next 10 customers: Who else matches your end user profile to validate your assumptions hold across multiple customers.

Group 2. (What can you do for your customer?)

  • Full life cycle use case: How does the customer discover acquire uses and gets value from your product in e2e lifecycle.
  • High level production specification: Outline what benefits this product brings to customers
  • Quantify the value proposition: Explain numerically how the customer will benefit from this
  • Define your core: Secret sauce/ competitive moat. E.g. Network effect; NOT a business model.
  • Competitive Position: Top 2 priorities from top customer from beach head market plotted against one another

Group 3. (How does your customer acquire your product?)

  • Decision making unit (DMU): What is required for a customer to say yes to something. E.g. Primary economic buyer, influencers, champion, (someone who wants your product bought, influencers, veto power, Purchasing department.
  • Map the process to acquire a paying customer: ?
  • Map the sales process to acquire a customer: How much money and time does it take to acquire a customer. Can this be shortened?

Group 4. (How do you make money off this product?)

  • Design a business model: Monthly payment, one time payment, ads, etc
  • Set your pricing framework: Calculate based on TLV and COCA from client. Also how much value the product adds.
  • Calculate LTV of customer: Life time value of a customer is based of recurring payments, etc
  • Calculate COCA: Cost of customer acquisition is calculated top down (total sales + market spend) / new customers

Group 5. (How do you design and build your product?)

  • Identify key assumptions: Profile and persona want to buy goods from X country
  • Test key assumptions: Cheapest way to test all assumptions
  • Define MVBP: How can we best include all assumptions to make testing efficient. Customer gets value, customer pays, and it starts feedback loop (literal feedback)
  • Show that dogs will eat dog food: Even though all the signals say that the profile and persona want to buy X goods, do they actually?

Group 6. (How do you scale your business?)

  • Develop a product (not just business) plan: Product evolution and market expansion past MVBP.
  • Calculate TAM for follow on markets: How much can your business expand for the next 10 customers (past beachhead market)
