September 2025 to April 2026. Wellington, then briefly Austin, Texas.

I wrote about twenty modules by hand first, to find out what a good one looked like, then built the multi-stage LLM pipeline that writes all of them now: brainstorm, staged research through MCP tools, then compilation against a large rule set into block-structured JSON. Dawn Aerospace, spacecraft propulsion development intern

November 2024 to February 2025. Four hundred hours, Christchurch.

Two projects. First, characterising a propellant feedline's thermal and electrical behaviour inside a vacuum chamber: a test jig, vacuum-safe cabling, a GUI over a local YAMCS server, and the analysis. HireOS

July 2026 to present. Live at five equipment-rental companies.

A rental management system for equipment hire companies, built and sold on my own.
