Wind load analysis, March 2024. report (pdf)

A wind load analysis on a stage cover in Autodesk CFD and SolidWorks, 10 to 60 km/h. The client's own tests and the simulation disagreed by 19% on safety margins, so the report reconciled the two and recommended changes to rope selection and mounting. I ran the whole thing: geometry, meshing, simulation setup, convergence studies, post-processing, and the write-up.
