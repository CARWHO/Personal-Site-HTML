HireOS

July 2026 to present. hireos.co.nz.

Every hire enquiry, in one place. One number for the whole team, every call and text in one app, synced to their rental system. Built and sold solo. Live at Accent Productions, The Rock Factory, Triac Light & Sound, Marquee Hire City, and NES Hire, with AV Media and Crown Events in the works.

Calls, texts and voicemails in one list, each under the customer.

Every call recorded and written up.

New jobs and changes synced to their rental system, waiting for the team to accept.

When the team is busy, the receptionist answers the call or text.

The important bits

  • 24 discovery interviews before selling anything. 17 named the same pain: enquiries arrive under-specified.
  • Intake: an AI receptionist answers over SIP, writes up each call and text, and drafts the job in Current RMS or Rentman. Changes wait for a person to accept.
  • RMS: for companies with no rental system. Quoting, approval, payment, and crewing, with gear booked under advisory locks so two jobs can't take the same kit. Numbers land in Xero.
  • Mobile: the app in a Capacitor shell with calling written natively. PJSIP over TLS to Asterisk, Android ConnectionService, push for incoming calls and texts.
  • Go and Postgres on Cloud Run, React on Bun, Terraform, CI against a real Postgres, green on main deploys itself.
  • Since 9 July 2026: 1,133 commits, 627 pull requests, 169 issues across three repos. Code is private :)
