Kahu Hutton

KORA

Founder and lead engineer, 2024 to 2025. A Google-backed startup in 2025. Acqui-hired by NextWork.

KORA is an LMS plugin that generates and grades course content. RAG over the university's own material, so answers come from the course, not the model's general knowledge. Funded by Google Cloud. Entered in the Lee Kuan Yew Global Business Plan Competition, that's the submission video below.

I built the full-stack web app in React and Next.js, Dockerised, on cloud-hosted databases with auth built in, and set up automated cloud deploys. With Joel Bannister on backend and DevOps and Lev Petersen on the LMS plugin we shipped a standalone plugin and RAG status tracker that had 100+ daily users. I owned the product from concept and UI through to user feedback.

I also did the fundraising. I learnt a lot pitching to Icehouse Ventures (Mason Bleakley) and Blackbird Ventures. Investors do not ask about the tech. They ask who pays, how much, and why they would keep paying. That shaped how I went about HireOS.

KORA ended when NextWork acquired the team. That is how I ended up there as a product engineer haha.
