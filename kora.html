2024 to 2025. A Google-backed startup in 2025.

KORA is an LMS plugin that generates and grades course content. RAG over the university's own material, so answers come from the course, not the model's general knowledge. Funded by Google Cloud. Entered in the Lee Kuan Yew Global Business Plan Competition.

What I built

  • Full-stack web app in React and Next.js, Dockerised, cloud-hosted databases with auth built in.
  • Standalone LMS plugin and RAG status tracker, 100+ daily users, built with a team of three.
  • Automated cloud deploys.
  • Owned the product from concept and UI through to user feedback.
